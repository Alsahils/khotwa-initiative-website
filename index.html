About Us

A professional job shadowing program that provides university students with practical experience in their field of study by connecting them with leading companies in the Saudi market.

Students spend one week at a company's headquarters, observing professionals in their daily work environment and participating in real projects.

Our Purpose

To bridge the gap between academic education and professional practice by providing students with real-world work experience.

Connect Students with Industry

Create direct connections between university students and leading companies, facilitating knowledge exchange and networking opportunities.

Provide Professional Experience

Offer students a one-week job shadowing experience that simulates the professional work environment and applies theoretical knowledge.

Develop Workplace Skills

Enhance students' professional skills including communication, teamwork, and problem-solving through direct observation and participation.

Guide Career Choices

Help students make informed career decisions by exposing them to different professional paths and work environments before graduation.

Build Talent Pipeline

Enable companies to identify promising talent early and build relationships with potential future employees.

Bridge Education Gap

Address the gap between academic curriculum and industry requirements through practical application of knowledge.
